FO4SF separate-folder check
1. Wait for the PC helper to say the separate-folder test is prepared and ready.
2. Watch the helper open the prepared FO4SF copy of the game.
3. Check that the game reaches its main menu.
4. Stay on the main menu for this check.
5. Choose Quit to Desktop.
6. Wait for the helper to confirm the game has closed and finished checking your usual game and saves.
7. Tell the helper if the game did not reach the menu, closed unexpectedly, or showed an error.
8. Leave the prepared test files in place for the helper to check.
